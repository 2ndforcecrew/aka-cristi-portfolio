Visual Identity

AKA.CRISTI is a single eye behind two disciplines. The camera hunts posture, fabric, and the exact second light turns ordinary into expensive. The drawing board takes that second apart and rebuilds it into a system a brand can own — grids, type, rhythm, restraint.

Nothing here is decorated. Every frame is measured; every typeface is interrogated before it is allowed on the page. Photography is content. Typography is structure. Motion is behaviour. When all three obey the same rule, the result does not ask for attention — it commands it.

The portfolio moves between fashion editorials, campaign imagery, brand identities, and typographic experiments. The medium changes. The standard does not. From the first frame to the final deliverable, one hand controls the shutter and the layout, because precision is not a style here. It is the only option.

For commissions, campaigns, and visual identities that refuse to be ordinary — the door is open.
