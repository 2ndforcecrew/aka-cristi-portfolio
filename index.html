    04 / 06 — About

    AKA.CRISTI

    AKA.CRISTI is not a traditional photographer portfolio. It is an avant-garde fashion magazine, a design archive, and a single-brand gallery — cold, precise, and sharp.

    The work moves between two mediums: the camera chases light and posture; paper reconstructs that emotion into a visual language a brand can keep. Lens captures. Paper settles. Both share one aesthetic.

    From shutter to final piece, every step is controlled under the same eye — because the photographer and the designer are the same person.
